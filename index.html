⚡ Ohm's Law

Relationship between voltage, current and resistance.

V = I × R

Observe how changing V or R changes current and power.

🪐 Planetary Motion & Gravity

Explore how gravity governs planetary orbits and how orbital speed and period depend on distance and central mass.

F = GMm/r²   |   v = √(GM/r)

Compare planets, change central mass and inspect orbital measurements. The simulator uses an educational circular-orbit model.

🌍 Gravity

Objects accelerate downward because of gravitational acceleration.

v = gt   |   s = ½gt²

Compare objects and environments in the simulation.

🔍 Convex Lens

A converging lens forms images depending on focal length and object position.

1/f = 1/v − 1/u

Explore image distance, magnification and orientation.

🚀 Projectile Motion

Horizontal and vertical motions combine to form a trajectory.

x = u cosθ · t

Change speed, angle and gravity and compare range, height and time.

🧲 Electromagnetic Induction

A changing magnetic flux through a coil induces an EMF.

ε = −N · dΦ/dt

More turns or faster flux change generally increases induced EMF. Lenz’s law gives its direction.

🔌 Series–Parallel Circuits

Compare current, voltage drops, branch currents, equivalent resistance and power.

Series: Rₜ = ΣR   |   Parallel: 1/Rₜ = Σ(1/R)

Use the interactive lab to switch topologies and open/close branches.
